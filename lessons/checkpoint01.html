Checkpoint 1: Electronics for Programmers

How well have lessons 5 to 18 stuck? A quick test, one question at a time, with a result for every lesson of Part 1.

You find out which of lessons 5 to 18 have stuck: Ohm's law and power, dividers and meters, LEDs, transistor and MOSFET switches, capacitors and coils, supplies, logic levels, schematics and datasheets. Then you know what to go over again before the computer architecture lessons.

The checkpoint has 28 multiple-choice questions on lessons 5 to 18, one at a time. Each question draws on one to three of those lessons, and the result estimates how well each lesson has stuck, so you know what to go over again before Part 2. Answer from memory and keep going: speed is fine. When you do not know, press I don't know, because a lucky guess hides a lesson you should revisit. The right answers, with explanations, come at the end.

Below the checkpoint you can also build your own test from the bank of 28 questions: pick the lessons you want, for example the ones the checkpoint marked Go over again after you have reread them, and choose how many questions to answer.

The facts come from the RP2040 datasheet, and from the part datasheets each lesson links.
