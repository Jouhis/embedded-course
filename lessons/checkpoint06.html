Checkpoint 6: architecture, testing, analysis and robustness

A mixed review of all of Part 6, lessons 76 to 85. Answer without the lesson open, then check the reasons. Every wrong answer points at a lesson to revisit.

By the end of this checkpoint you can answer mixed questions on driver layers, active objects, memory, git review, unit tests, fakes, CI, static analysis, fault handling and field logs, and explain each one out loud.

The questions cover all ten lessons of Part 6, with at least two per lesson from 76 to 80. Older lessons appear only where Part 6 depends on them.

Explain out loud

Say each answer for 60 seconds, without notes, then check your explanation against the lessons named.

  1. Walk through what happens between a fault and the first line of your HardFault handler. Name the stack the frame lands on, the EXC_RETURN bit that tells you which one, and what the naked stub does before it jumps to C (lesson 84).
  2. A driver test passes on a starter where nothing works yet. Explain how that can happen with a fake bus (lesson 81), and how red, green, refactor (lesson 80) would have shown the gap before the code was written.
  3. Explain why a driver layer, a backend chosen at link time (lesson 76) and an active object with its own queue (lesson 77) together let you test most of a product on a PC without a lock. Say which part still needs the board.
