How well have lessons 45 to 50 stuck? A quick test, one question at a time, with a result for every lesson of Part 3.

You find out which of lessons 45 to 50 have stuck and which to go over again before the peripheral lessons, where every driver uses these rules on real registers.

The checkpoint has 19 multiple-choice questions on lessons 45 to 50, one at a time. Each question draws on one to three of those lessons, and the result estimates how well each lesson has stuck, so you know what to go over again before Part 4. Answer from memory and keep going: speed is fine. When you do not know, press I don't know, because a lucky guess hides a lesson you should revisit. The right answers, with explanations, come at the end.

Below the checkpoint you can also build your own test from the bank of 60 questions: pick the lessons you want, for example the ones the checkpoint marked Go over again after you have reread them, and choose how many questions to answer.

The facts come from cppreference, implicit conversions and volatile, and RM0008 for the GPIO registers. Each lesson lists its own sources.
