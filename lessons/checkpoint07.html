Explain out loud

Say each answer in about two minutes, as if to an interviewer, then open the notes. Use the notes as a guide and say the answer in your own words.

  1. Explain why the Lab 6 threads wait at a start gate before reading any device, and what would go wrong without it.
  2. Walk through what happens from a button press on GP14 to the LED changing, naming each thread, queue and driver call in the Lab 6 port.
  3. Explain the difference between a Zephyr priority and a FreeRTOS priority, and say what you would check first when porting a FreeRTOS task to a Zephyr thread.
Notes for the three prompts

For 1: the threads start from K_THREAD_DEFINE before main() finishes, and they would read devices that are not initialised. For 2: the button thread posts to button_q, the UI thread wakes, calls ui_poll, which debounces the level from hal_gpio_read, and then ui_draw and the LED write happen on the next draw. For 3: the numbers run the other way, and a larger FreeRTOS number is more urgent, so a copied constant can invert the priorities.
