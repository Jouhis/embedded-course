You find out which of lessons 100 to 106 have stuck: how the buses decide who talks and how a receiver checks a message, how a bootloader and a flash log survive a power cut, and how a device is protected. Then you know what to go over again before the signal-processing lessons.

The checkpoint has 21 multiple-choice questions on lessons 100 to 106, one at a time. Each question draws on one to three of those lessons, and the result estimates how well each lesson has stuck, so you know what to go over again before Part 9. Answer from memory and keep going: speed is fine. When you do not know, press I don't know, because a lucky guess hides a lesson you should revisit. The right answers, with explanations, come at the end.

Below the checkpoint you can also build your own test from the bank of 70 questions: pick the lessons you want, for example the ones the checkpoint marked Go over again after you have reread them, and choose how many questions to answer.
