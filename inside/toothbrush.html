Inside an electric toothbrush

One button, one MOSFET, PWM and sleep

A button, a motor, an LED and a battery around one microcontroller that spends nearly all its life asleep. Click the round button on the handle, or the one in the toolbar.

Things to try

  • Wake it upPress the button. Watch IDD jump from microamps to milliamps as the chip leaves Stop mode, then to the motor current once the MOSFET switches.
  • Change the modePress again while it runs. The PWM duty drops and the motor slows; a third press switches it off and the chip goes back to sleep.
  • Bump itA short knock wakes the chip, but the button does not read low three times in a row, so the firmware goes straight back to sleep.
  • Low batteryDrain the battery and press. The ADC reading is below the limit, so the motor never starts and the LED blinks red.
