Inside a fitness tracker

Sleep, interrupts and a battery budget

A tracker spends almost all its time asleep. It wakes for a few milliseconds when the accelerometer, the button or a timer asks, and how often that happens decides how many days the battery lasts.

Things to try

  • Poll or FIFOSwitch between Poll 20 ms and FIFO + INT1 and watch the CPU lane and the days of battery. Same steps counted, very different battery life.
  • Go for a walkStart walking at 10× and watch the step count. Every 100 steps the vibration motor runs, and the current jumps.
  • Raise your wristIn FIFO mode the display can come on up to half a second late, because the samples wait in the FIFO. Polling sees it at once, at a price.
  • Leave the screen onPress the button again and again. The display costs more than the processor ever does.
