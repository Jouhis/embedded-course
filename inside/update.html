Inside a firmware update

Two slots, a CRC and a bootloader

A device takes a new firmware image while it keeps running the old one. The flash map shows where every sector goes, and you can pull the power at any moment.

Things to try

  • A normal updateSend an update. The other slot is erased and written sector by sector, the CRC is checked, and after a trial boot the new image confirms itself.
  • Pull the powerUnplug in the middle of the download, then plug it in. The half-written slot is ignored and the device boots the old image.
  • A bit flipsFlip a bit on the wire and send. Every sector arrives, but the CRC differs from the header's, so the image is never marked pending.
  • A broken buildSend a broken build. It passes the CRC but crashes. After two trial boots the bootloader rolls back to the slot that worked.
