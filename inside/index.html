Look inside

Everyday devices, opened up and animated

Every device on this page has a microcontroller in it doing the kind of work this course teaches. Each page lets you play with the device and watch what happens inside: the pins, the signals, the firmware and the messages it sends. You need no background for them, and every term links to the lesson that teaches it, so they work as a preview before Part 1 and as a reminder later.

  • 1Inside a keyboarda scanned key matrix, pull-ups, debounce, ghost keys and diodes, the USB report
  • 2Inside a kettlea thermistor divider, the ADC, a relay heater with slow PWM, PI keep-warm, safety cutoffs
  • 3Inside a bottling linelight beams on an edge interrupt, encoder tracking, a reject queue and solenoid, Modbus RTU
  • 4Inside a fitness trackerWFI sleep, an accelerometer FIFO and its interrupt, I2C, average current and battery life
  • 5Inside a 3D printera host and a move queue, step pulses from a timer, acceleration, homing, lost steps
  • 6Inside a car's brakeswheel-speed nodes on CAN, arbitration, anti-lock control of the brake valves, a sensor timeout
  • 7Inside a microwave ovena keypad, a state machine, slow PWM of a relay, a hardware door interlock, a watchdog
  • 8Inside an elevatorcall buttons, floor sensors, PWM motor, brake, a state machine, door safety
  • 9Inside a firmware updatecoming · two slots, a CRC check, pulling the power
  • 10Inside an electric toothbrushcoming · one button, one MOSFET, PWM and sleep
