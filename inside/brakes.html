Inside a car's brakes

Wheel-speed nodes, CAN and anti-lock braking

Each wheel has a small node that reports its speed over a shared CAN bus. The brake controller compares the four speeds and, when a wheel starts to lock, lets the pressure off that wheel for a moment.

Things to try

  • Brake hardPress the brake on a dry road. The front-left valve releases and re-applies many times a second, and the wheel keeps turning.
  • Switch ABS offDrive at 100 km/h again and brake with ABS off. The wheels lock, the tyres turn red, and the stopping distance grows.
  • Try iceOn ice the grip is a sixth of a dry road. ABS cannot shorten the stop much, but it keeps the front wheels turning, so the car can still steer.
  • Unplug a sensorThe FL frames stop and the panel shows one node fewer in arbitration. After 50 ms the controller gives up ABS and lights the warning lamp.
