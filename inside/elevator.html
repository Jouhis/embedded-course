Inside an elevator

Buttons, sensors, a motor and a state machine

One microcontroller runs this elevator. On the left is the shaft and everything wired to the chip. On the right the chip is opened up, so you can see the firmware's state and variables change as the car moves.

Things to try

  • Call from two floorsPress 3, then 1 while the car passes floor 1 on its way up. Watch the calls bits and the order the car serves them.
  • Block the doorsPress "Step into the doorway" while the doors are closing. The light curtain cuts the beam and the state machine reopens.
  • Pull the emergency stopStop the car between floors and watch BRAKE_RELEASE and MOTOR_PWM drop together, then Reset.
  • Watch the PWMSet the speed to ½× to see the duty cycle ramp up when the car starts and down as it nears the floor.
