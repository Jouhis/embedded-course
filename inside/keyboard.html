Inside a keyboard

A scanned key matrix, debounce and a USB report

Sixteen keys share eight pins: the microcontroller drives one row low at a time and reads which columns follow it. Click the keys or type on your own keyboard, and watch the scan, the debounce counters and the bytes that go to the host.

Things to try

  • Slow motionAt 1/1000× hold one key and watch the contact chatter, while the orange bar under the key fills only once 10 scans agree.
  • Make a ghostPress Q + W + A with the diodes off. S lights up red although nobody pressed it, and the report turns to 01 01 01 01 01 01.
  • Fit the diodesSwitch Diodes on and press Q + W + A again. Only the three real keys reach the host.
  • Seven keysThe boot report has room for six keys. Press 7 keys and watch the firmware report ErrorRollOver instead.
