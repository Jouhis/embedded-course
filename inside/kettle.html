Inside a kettle

A thermistor, the ADC and a PWM heater

The microcontroller reads the water temperature through a thermistor and the ADC, and switches the heater with a relay. Boil a litre, keep it warm at 70 °C, and see what the firmware does when something goes wrong.

Things to try

  • Boil itPress the button with the target at 100°. Watch the ADC code fall as the water warms, and the heater stop once the reading levels off.
  • Keep it warmPick 70° and press the button. Full power at first, then the duty cycle drops and the relay clicks on and off to hold the temperature.
  • Boil it dryPour the water out and press the button. With no water the element heats fast, and at 110 °C the firmware cuts it and enters FAULT.
  • Unplug the sensorThe ADC reads 4095, a code no real temperature gives, so the firmware refuses to heat until the sensor is back and you press Reset.
