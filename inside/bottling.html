Inside a bottling line

Light beams, an encoder and a reject solenoid

Bottles pass two light beams, and the controller has to push the underfilled ones off the belt a moment later, further down the line. A PLC reads the counts over Modbus every second.

Things to try

  • Make a bad oneClick Next one underfilled. The ISR puts its position in the queue, and the solenoid fires when the encoder count gets there.
  • Change the belt speedWith tracking by encoder, change the speed or stop the belt. The right bottle is still pushed, because the count moves only when the belt does.
  • Track by timeSwitch to Time and go Slow or Fast. The fixed 2 s wait now misses, and full bottles end up in the reject bin.
  • Read the framesThe PLC's request is the same frame as in the Modbus lesson, CRC C4 0B. The reply carries the two counters, big-endian.
