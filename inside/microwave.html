Inside a microwave oven

A state machine, a door interlock and a watchdog

A keypad, a door switch and four relays around one microcontroller. The firmware is a small state machine, and two safety nets sit outside it: a door switch wired in hardware and a watchdog timer.

Things to try

  • Heat somethingPress +30 a few times, then start. At half power the magnetron relay goes on and off every few seconds, which is slow PWM.
  • Open the doorOpen it while cooking. The firmware pauses, and the second switch has already cut the magnetron in hardware.
  • Freeze the firmwareStart cooking and freeze it. The watchdog counter climbs, and after 100 ms the chip resets with every output off.
  • Without the watchdogSwitch the watchdog off and freeze again. The magnetron keeps running, so open the door or unplug the oven before the food burns.
