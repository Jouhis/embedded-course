Inside a 3D printer

Step pulses, a move queue and homing

A host computer plans the moves and the microcontroller turns each one into step pulses from a timer interrupt. The firmware only counts the steps it sends, so it believes the head is where the count says.

Things to try

  • Home, then printPress Home X, then Start print. Each pass lays down a layer, and the bed drops by one layer height.
  • Turn the ramp offWith Ramp off and the feed at 150 or 250 mm/s, each move starts with lost steps. The count and the real head drift apart, and the layers shift.
  • Home againAfter a shift, stop and press Home X. The endstop puts the count right, which is why every print starts by homing.
  • Pause the hostThe 8-move queue keeps the head going for a while, then it runs dry and the head stops in the middle of the part.
