Ohm's law and power

QuantityFormulaLesson
Ohm's lawV = I × R, so I = V ÷ R and R = V ÷ I5
PowerP = V × I = I² × R = V² ÷ R (watts)6
EnergyE = P × t (joules, with t in seconds; 1 Wh = 3600 J)6
Rating checkDissipated P must be well below the rated power; add margin, and apply the datasheet derating above its rated temperature6

Series, parallel, and Kirchhoff

ConnectionRuleLesson
SeriesSame current in each part. Rtotal = R1 + R2 + …7
ParallelSame voltage across each part. 1/Rtotal = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + …; for two, R1 × R2 ÷ (R1 + R2)7
KCLAt a node, the currents in equal the currents out7
KVLAround a loop, the voltage drops sum to the source voltage7

E12 preferred values

Multiply each value by 1, 10, 100, and so on (for example 4.7 Ω, 47 Ω, 470 Ω, 4.7 kΩ). Standard: IEC 60063.
