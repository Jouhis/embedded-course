MISRA C quick list

How the MISRA C:2012 rules are organised, what the tools in this course check, and what they do not. No rule texts are reproduced here: the MISRA standard is licensed.

Before you write a deviation

  • Can the code be changed instead? Most deviations are a sign of a design problem.
  • Record the rule, the location, the reason, and the reviewer. A deviation without a record is a defect in an audit.
  • Never deviate a mandatory rule.
