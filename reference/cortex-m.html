Exception entry frame (eight words)

Stacked by the coreRestored on return
PC, xPSR, R0 to R3, R12, LRAll eight, by unstacking

Entry latency: 12 cycles from asserting the interrupt to the first ISR instruction (DDI0337G §5.5, Figure 5-2). Tail-chaining (§5.6) and late arrival (§5.7) change the sequence. STKALIGN (§5.5.1) can add one padding word.
